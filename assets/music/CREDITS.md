# Music credits

All music in this folder is in the public domain, so it can be used, modified and
shared without permission or attribution. Credits are listed as a courtesy.

The background tracks come from [FreePD.com](https://freepd.com), a library of music
dedicated to the public domain (CC0). FreePD closed in 2025; its catalog is preserved
on the Internet Archive at https://archive.org/details/freepd. Each track was trimmed
to 85 seconds with a fade out and loudness normalized for Retrocast.
